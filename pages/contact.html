Quick Answers

How do I start a project with OTI-TECH?

Fill out the contact form above or message us on WhatsApp. We will schedule a brief call to understand your goals and outline the next steps.

Do you work outside Lagos?

Yes. We deliver design, construction and real estate support across Nigeria. Remote coordination and site visits are part of our standard process.

What information should I prepare before contacting you?

Any details you have: site location, budget range, timeline, type of project (residential, commercial, land), and any sketches or references. If you don't have all of this yet, we will help you build the brief.

How are fees structured?

Design fees are typically fixed or percentage-based depending on scope. Construction works on a cost-plus or fixed-price basis after design is complete. Real estate fees follow standard agency terms. We provide clear proposals before any commitment.
