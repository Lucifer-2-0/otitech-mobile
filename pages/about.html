Our story

Built around your vision, grounded in the real world.

OTI-TECH was founded on a simple observation: property decisions are too often fragmented. Designers don't talk to builders. Agents don't understand construction. Clients get caught in the middle.

We set out to change that. By integrating architectural design, construction execution and real estate advisory under one roof, we help clients move from idea to finished property with fewer handoffs, clearer accountability and better outcomes.

Our approach is practical, not theoretical. Every design considers buildability and cost. Every construction project is managed to a programme and budget. Every property recommendation is grounded in market reality and due diligence.
