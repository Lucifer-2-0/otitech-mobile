HOW TO READ THIS

A useful range.
Not a final quote.

The planner scales an illustrative 140 m² three-bedroom bungalow bill of quantities against dated Nigerian material, labour and plant rates. It adjusts materials, labour and plant for the selected location, and applies basic, standard or premium roof, tile and ceiling rates. Multi-storey projects include an indicative allowance for upper-floor structure.

The reference quantities are not your drawings. Soil conditions, structural design, lifts, fire systems, external works, approvals, professional fees and furnishing can change the real cost. High-rise projects need a project-specific quantity survey and structural design before a reliable budget can be set.

Rates are updated by the source periodically, not tick-by-tick. Check the displayed rate date, request local supplier quotes, and get a measured bill of quantities before committing funds.
